## Full Description
SpenIt AICore is a smart finance tracker for everyday spending, income, receipts, bank statements and tax relief.

The app has been rebuilt around a cleaner dashboard that shows what matters quickly: safe-to-spend balance, daily and weekly totals, income context, recent expenses, tax-deductible totals and AI-powered insights. Add records manually, scan documents, or share images and PDFs into SpenIt AICore when you want the app to help extract the details.

AI features can run through Google AICore on supported devices, with optional provider settings for Gemini, OpenAI or an OpenAI-compatible endpoint if you choose to configure them. Manual tracking still works even when AI extraction is unavailable.

Key features:
- Clean dashboard with safe-to-spend, income, daily average and recent activity
- Receipt scanning for expense extraction and categorisation
- Payslip scanning and income tracking
- Shared import queue for images, PDFs and bank statements
- Malaysian bank statement import for expense and income entries
- Tax Relief view with deductible expense totals and ZIP export
- Search and filters for expenses and income
- AI insights for summaries, category trends and saving tips
- Google Drive backup and restore
- Google sign-in, passkeys and biometric unlock

SpenIt AICore is designed as a personal record-keeping tool. It does not connect to your bank account directly and it does not provide financial, tax or investment advice. Always review extracted records before relying on them.

## Data Safety Notes To Review In Play Console
- Financial info: expenses, income, receipts, and related records
- Photos/files: receipt images and PDFs imported by the user
- Personal info/account: Google sign-in account details
- Device identifiers or advertising ID: AdMob is configured, so review whether the production build collects ad identifiers
- Data transfer/security: describe Google Drive backup and sign-in behavior accurately
- AI processing: describe optional remote AI providers if users can configure external API keys
- Account deletion: Settings includes account deletion; ensure Play Console data deletion answers match the implemented flow
