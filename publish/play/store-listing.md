## Full Description
SpenIt AICore helps you track everyday spending, scan receipts, review income, and understand where your money is going.

Capture expenses from receipts and shared files, organize spending by category, and keep an eye on your balance with clear dashboard summaries. Built around privacy-first, on-device AI workflows, SpenIt AICore helps turn financial records into useful insights without making expense tracking feel heavy.

Key features:
- Scan receipts and extract expense details
- Track income, daily spending, and recent expenses
- Review category trends and spending insights
- Import shared images and PDFs
- Back up your data with Google Drive
- Sign in with Google, passkeys, or biometrics
