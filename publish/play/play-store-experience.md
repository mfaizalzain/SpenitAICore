# SpenIt AICore Play Store Experience

## Positioning
SpenIt AICore is no longer just a receipt scanner. The Store experience should present it as a compact personal finance workspace: scan receipts, track income, import bank statements, review AI insights, and prepare tax relief records.

## Lead Message
Track money faster with AI-assisted records for expenses, income, receipts, bank statements, and tax relief.

## Visual Direction
- Use the refreshed app UI as the hero, not abstract finance artwork.
- Lead with the Dashboard because it now communicates the product clearly: safe-to-spend, income, daily average, tax totals, and recent records.
- Keep screenshots practical and legible. Each frame should show real app controls and sample Malaysian Ringgit data.
- Prefer short caption overlays when preparing Play screenshots. The caption should explain the outcome, while the phone UI proves it.

## Feature Graphic
Use a single focused composition:

- Left: app name and short value line, "AI-assisted expense, income and tax records"
- Right: cropped Dashboard phone screenshot with the safe-to-spend card visible
- Accent callouts: "Receipt scan", "Bank import", "Tax export"
- Avoid overloading the graphic with every feature; it should make the app feel current and trustworthy.
